# The Vendor–AOO Seam

The Command Broker is the one role in the corpus that **no single stakeholder fully owns.** The human broker is the asset owner/operator's; the enforcement mechanism, the confirmation interface, the exercise mode, and the audit records are the vendor's; and band classification is shared. Neither side can implement the role alone.

The corpus assigns each half of these obligations well — but the **handoff** by which a qualified broker is actually stood up on a specific product at a specific facility is sequenced by the **CB-CT Commissioning &amp; Joint Training** module. That sequence is [the Engagement Journey](../journey/overview.md): six gates, a shared responsibility model, a joint training event, and a model-update re-qualification loop.

This site exists to make that seam walkable from both sides at once.
