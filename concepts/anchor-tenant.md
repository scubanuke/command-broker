# Compliance as Anchor Tenant

In a regulated critical-infrastructure enterprise, mandatory compliance spend is the **anchor tenant** that capitalizes the data substrate on which every other AI-supported process rides.

Compliance with NERC and, at a nuclear plant, NRC obligations is a non-discretionary spend: the organization must build a normalized data store, provenance machinery, an on-premises retrieval corpus, and an evidence apparatus — whatever it costs. That substrate has to exist regardless. So every adjacent process that can ride on it — engineering trending, work-order support, operations intelligence — rides at **marginal cost**. Compliance is the department store whose guaranteed lease gets the mall built; the specialty shops live off the foot traffic.

Two guardrails keep the argument honest. **Cost allocation:** compliance is funded and fully received; adjacent uses pay only their increment — the same burn, or an incrementally increased one. **Security floor:** the compliance tenant sets the controls for the whole building, so a low-consequence use cannot corrupt or leak a high-consequence tenant's guarantees.
