# Mechanism vs. Accountability

The vendor may **build and operate** the enforcement mechanism — the confirmation interface, the exercise mode, the audit records. But **accountability for the brokered decision** — and therefore for the broker's fitness to make it — remains with the asset owner/operator's named human broker.

> There is no chair at Nuremberg for an organization.

Accountability does not flow to the AI system, nor to the AI supplier. A vendor's characterization of a product — "turnkey autonomy," "operator-free optimization" — does not substitute for gate evidence and does not, by itself, close any gate. This is the principle that lets the [Vendor–AOO Seam](vendor-aoo-seam.md) divide cleanly: the vendor is *responsible* for the mechanism; the AOO is *accountable* for the decision.
