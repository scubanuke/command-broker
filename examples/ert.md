# Worked Example · The ERT

The **Evidence Request Tool (ERT)** is a companion that automates NERC CIP audit evidence: an extract-and-normalize substrate across source systems, provenance tracking that keeps an unbroken chain of custody, a versioned evidence store, retrieval-augmented answers with citations, hallucination validation, mandatory human-review gates, and signed evidence packages.

Read through the Command Broker lens, the ERT sits **below the Bright Line** — it assembles and holds evidence; a named human signs. Its human-review gate *is* the Bright Line expressed inside the tool; its provenance machinery *is* the reconstructable record a broker relies on.
