# Worked Example · Nuclear SE Support AI

A nuclear system engineer stands up one AI tool used by three groups — system-performance assessment, maintenance work-order development, and operations system testing — across a five-unit fleet. It is the framework at its highest-consequence setting.

The single tool carries **four broker seams**: the SE (assessment, below the line), the maintenance approver (work orders on safety-related SSCs, above the line), the licensed operator (test execution against Technical Specifications, above the line), and the executive owning fleet allocation (advisory, but consequential). Those four seams are governed as a **role-based access model** co-authored by IT and OT, and the tool is commissioned through the same six [engagement gates](../journey/overview.md) — under 10 CFR 50 Appendix B quality, with a 50.59-style screen for model updates.
