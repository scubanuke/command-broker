# The Corpus

This site is a navigator, not an archive. The authoritative documents live on the **[Publications mirror](https://scubanuke.github.io/publications/)** — permanent, citable copies with stable URLs that survive revisions. Several are drafts. Versions shown are the texts as published. Where a later working draft exists and has not yet been published, the published text is a dated draft: it states the framework as of its version and is not the current statement. Newer versions will be posted here when they are released.

## Foundational Definitions & Determinations

The governing layer — the definitions the rest of the corpus conforms to, and the determinations that resolve its open questions. All three of FD-BL's open questions are now determined. Versions shown are those of the October 2026 Edition of the Foundational Definitions, issued 2 October 2026 (DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23117612](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23117612)).

- **FD-BL — The Bright Line** (v0.5) — the definition: placement (consequence, per action), what one action is, and the obligation; it cites FD-LD for scope.
- **FD-LD — Layer Decomposition & Determinism** (v0.4) — companion root; states scope as the complement of the determinism carve-out, and owns the layer vocabulary.
- **FD-BL-D1 — Mode of Discharge** (v0.5) — advance discharge by the Command Broker, with a verified gatekeeper admitting under R1–R3; the two-object model; the reach clause. See [Advance Discharge](concepts/advance-discharge.md).
- **FD-BL-D2 — Indication Integrity** (v0.4) — traceable, independently corroborated inputs (I1–I3), reaching placement and discharge inputs. See [Indication Integrity](concepts/indication-integrity.md).
- **FD-EV — Envelope** (v0.2) — reserves "envelope" for the authorization sense; renames the operational sense to "operating band". See [Envelope](concepts/envelope.md).
