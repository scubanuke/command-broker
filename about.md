# How to Use This Site

This site maps the practices by which an **asset owner/operator (AOO)** and a **vendor/supplier** engage to put a governed generative-AI tool into service in a critical-infrastructure setting. It sits over the Command Broker corpus — the framework, standards, and companions — and turns them into a walkable path rather than a stack of documents.

## One spine, two lenses

The heart of the site is **[the Engagement Journey](journey/overview.md)**: the six lifecycle gates from the CB-CT Commissioning &amp; Joint Training module. Every gate page shows the same event from two sides — an **AOO lens** and a **vendor lens**. The **Lens** switch in the sidebar filters what you see; entering a track door presets it.

## Provenance &amp; currency

This is a navigator, not an archive. The authoritative documents live in the Command Broker Drive folder, and the pages here link out to those canonical copies rather than re-hosting them — see **[The Corpus](corpus.md)**. Version and status are shown per document; several are drafts.
