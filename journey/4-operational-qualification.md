# Gate 4 · Operational Qualification

**Entry.** Named brokers assigned; SAT closed. **Activities.** The **Joint Training Event** is delivered, and each assigned broker demonstrates CB-IB competency on this specific product. The tool's **roles are defined here** — a role-based access model co-authored by IT and OT. **Exit.** A per-broker competency record and the training-event package. **This gate closes individually, per broker.** **Accountable.** The AOO — no broker operates above the Bright Line until it has closed for that individual.

<div class="lens lens-aoo">

<p class="lenstag">Asset Owner / Operator</p>

**You do.** Staff, qualify, and maintain the human brokers. Define the role-based access model: **IT** provides the system's home (compute, identity/access, network); **OT** provides its data and the process semantics; your governance authority is accountable for whether the roles correctly place the Bright Line. Bind roles to competency records, not to persons, and enforce separation of duties and approver independence.

</div>

<div class="lens lens-vendor">

<p class="lenstag">Vendor / Supplier</p>

**You deliver.** The vendor-led half of the Joint Training Event — the product-specific interface and exercise-mode walkthrough — so the AOO's brokers can be qualified against how *this* product behaves.

</div>
