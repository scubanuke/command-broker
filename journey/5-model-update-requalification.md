# Gate 5 · Model-Update Re-Qualification

**Entry.** A vendor material-change notice. **Activities.** Classify the change; where material, re-run the affected Joint Training Event modules and re-qualify the affected brokers. **Exit.** A re-qualification record plus a confirmation of audit continuity across the change. **Accountable.** The AOO.

**Material-change test.** A change is material if it could alter the product's outputs for any class of actions at or above the Bright Line, or if it changes the confirmation interface, the exercise mode, or the structure of the audit record. This is the governance analogue of a change-control screen — the point at which broker competency can silently go stale.

<div class="lens lens-aoo">

<p class="lenstag">Asset Owner / Operator</p>

**You do.** Screen each notified change against the material-change test. Re-qualify affected brokers before the updated product runs above the Bright Line. Preserve audit continuity so pre- and post-change decisions remain reconstructable from a coherent record.

</div>

<div class="lens lens-vendor">

<p class="lenstag">Vendor / Supplier</p>

**You deliver.** Advance notice of model, fine-tuning, and retrieval-source changes, and you re-run the affected training modules. Changes confined to cosmetic behavior with no effect on above-line outputs or on the evidence artifacts are non-material.

</div>
