# Gate 6 · Periodic Drill

**Entry.** The review interval or a triggering condition is reached — not less than annually for Tier 1 applications. **Activities.** A degraded-mode / adversarial-stress exercise covering the framework's stress categories (sensor-feed loss, comms degradation, elevated tempo, prompt injection, sensor-feed falsification). **Exit.** Drill findings logged; any competency lapse triggers remediation before continued above-Bright-Line operation. **Accountable.** The AOO.
