# Gate 1 · Procurement

**Entry.** The AOO has identified the *tier* of the intended application — the consequence of its worst credible error. **Exit.** A signed vendor tier-design statement and an accepted conformance clause are on file. **Accountable.** The AOO, on a joint tier determination with the vendor.

Tier classification is an application-level judgment made here: it fixes whether the Command Broker architecture is required at all. See **[Three-Tier Criticality](../concepts/three-tier.md)**.

<div class="lens lens-aoo">

<p class="lenstag">Asset Owner / Operator</p>

**You do.** Identify the tier from the consequence of the worst credible error. Accept — or negotiate — the conformance, audit-record, and audit-reconstruction clauses. Own the joint tier determination.

**You require from the vendor.** A tier-design statement, and the configuration changes needed to deploy the product at a different tier.

</div>

<div class="lens lens-vendor">

<p class="lenstag">Vendor / Supplier</p>

**You deliver.** The tier-design statement (DBA-VC §2.2) and the configuration path to deploy at a different tier. You accept the conformance and audit-reconstruction clauses (DBA-VC §3.1, §3.7).

**You cannot.** Substitute a product characterization for this evidence. A "turnkey" claim closes nothing.

</div>
