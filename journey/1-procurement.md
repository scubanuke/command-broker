# Gate 1 · Procurement

**Entry.** The AOO has identified the *band* of the intended application — the consequence of its worst credible error. **Exit.** A signed vendor band-design statement and an accepted conformance clause are on file. **Accountable.** The AOO, on a joint band determination with the vendor.

Band classification is an application-level judgment made here: it fixes whether the Command Broker architecture is required at all. See **[Criticality Bands](../concepts/three-tier.md)**.

<div class="lens lens-aoo">

<p class="lenstag">Asset Owner / Operator</p>

**You do.** Identify the band from the consequence of the worst credible error. Accept — or negotiate — the conformance, audit-record, and audit-reconstruction clauses. Own the joint band determination.

**You require from the vendor.** A band-design statement, and the configuration changes needed to deploy the product at a different band.

</div>

<div class="lens lens-vendor">

<p class="lenstag">Vendor / Supplier</p>

**You deliver.** The band-design statement (DBA-VC §2.2) and the configuration path to deploy at a different band. You accept the conformance and audit-reconstruction clauses (DBA-VC §3.1, §3.7).

**You cannot.** Substitute a product characterization for this evidence. A "turnkey" claim closes nothing.

</div>
